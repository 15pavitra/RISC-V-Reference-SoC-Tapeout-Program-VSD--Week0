Post-Synthesis GLS & STA Fundamentals
Objective

Perform Gate-Level Simulation (GLS) after synthesis.

Validate functionality of BabySoC design post-synthesis.

Learn the basics of Static Timing Analysis (STA) using OpenSTA.

Part 1 – Gate-Level Simulation (GLS)

GLS verifies the synthesized netlist (vsdbabysoc.synth.v).

Timing information is annotated using SDF files to ensure real-world correctness.

Waveforms are analyzed with GTKWave to check module interactions (RISC-V processor, PLL, DAC).

Detects glitches, race conditions, or metastability issues not visible at RTL level.

Part 2 – Fundamentals of STA (Static Timing Analysis)

STA analyzes timing paths of a design without simulation.

Focus Areas:

Setup and Hold Checks: Ensure signals arrive within valid windows relative to clock edges.

Slack: Positive slack indicates safe timing; negative slack indicates violations.

Clock Definitions: Period, duty cycle, and multiple clock domains affect timing analysis.

Path-Based Analysis: Evaluates individual paths to identify worst-case delays.
